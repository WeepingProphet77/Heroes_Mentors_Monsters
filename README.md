# Heroes, Mentors, and Monsters

A church class for older teens and adults on the structure of reality, the stories that endure, and the Logos who stands behind both. Based on Scripture, with help from Jonathan Pageau, Jordan B. Peterson, C.S. Lewis, and J.R.R. Tolkien.

## What's here

- `GUIDANCE.md` governs all work on this project. Read it first.
- `index.html` is the entire course presentation. Open it in any browser; it works offline. Arrow keys or spacebar advance slides, Escape returns to the menu.
- `images/` holds all artwork. `images/MANIFEST.md` lists every needed image with art direction; images are generated by ChatGPT to those exact filenames.
- `notes/` holds the teacher notes, one Word document per session.

## Status

**Complete.** All eleven sessions are built: 136 slides, 70 images, and eleven teacher notes documents. Every image in the manifest has been delivered and placed.

## The eleven sessions

1. The World Has a Shape (Genesis 1; John 1:1-5)
2. Why Stories Ring True (Ecclesiastes 3:11; Acts 17)
3. The Call of the Hero (Genesis 12; Exodus 3; 1 Samuel 16)
4. The Mentor (1 Samuel 3; 2 Timothy 1)
5. The Monster Outside (1 Samuel 17; Job 41)
6. The Monster Within (Genesis 4:7; Romans 7)
7. Into the Belly of the Beast (Jonah 1-2; Matthew 12:39-40)
8. The Life Laid Down (Isaiah 53; John 10:11-18; John 15:13)
9. The Least Likely One (1 Samuel 16:7; 1 Corinthians 1:26-29)
10. The Return of the King (Isaiah 9:6-7; Revelation 21)
11. Living Inside the Story (Hebrews 12:1-2; Ephesians 2:10)

## Teaching it

Open `index.html` and press a session tile. Arrow keys, spacebar, or click advance slides; Escape returns to the menu. The address bar tracks your position (for example `#s03/5`), so you can bookmark a slide or resume after a refresh. Each session's notes document includes preparation, a minute-by-minute timeline, talking points keyed to slide numbers, Scripture in full, two tiers of discussion questions, background reading, and pastoral notes.
